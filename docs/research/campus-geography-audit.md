# Campus geography and housing audit

Audit and implementation: October 24, 2026 (America/New_York). Public map retrieval: October 25, 2026, 02:25:09 UTC. This records geographic evidence and authored simulation choices separately.

The former map did **not** arbitrarily classify academic halls as homes: it contained three OSM dormitories, five houses and five apartment features. The problems were the tiny extent, clipped destinations, proxies, uniform capacities, and population/itinerary rules. The behavior audit owns population mix and schedules.

## Why this neighborhood and population interpretation

[Ohio State's neighborhood guide](https://offcampus.osu.edu/resources/community-resources/columbus-neighborhoods) describes the University District as predominantly undergraduate and graduate residents with a smaller long-term population, and notes that older houses are frequently subdivided. That supports a student-heavy scene and a small number of fictional local families; it does not supply exact percentages for this rectangle. The requested one or two families is a scenario choice, not a measured census.

[OSU's location-sector guide](https://offcampus.osu.edu/myoffcampus/LocationSectorMap.aspx) places the central University District east of High between 13th and 18th and distinguishes nearby walkable neighborhoods from farther commuter areas. The expansion covers that central residential relationship without requiring a whole-city map.

The [official OSU residence search](https://housing.osu.edu/hms/roomsearch?campus=1&view=map) lists Baker, Bradley, Paterson, German House, and Park-Stradley as residence halls. The [official residence map](https://housing.osu.edu/posts/documents/residence-hall-map-2024-2025.pdf) is a cross-check for names and relative locations; its date must not be mistaken for the current map extraction date. OSM tags plus explicit official overrides provide building categories; no real resident identities, household membership, or faculty home locations were collected.

Name matching also needs care: [Kuhn Honors and Scholars House](https://honors-scholars.osu.edu/about/kuhn-house) is an academic center, while [Scholars West](https://housing.osu.edu/hms/roomsearch/scholars-west?campus=1) is a residence hall. The loader uses the specific residence identity rather than interpreting the word “Scholars” as housing evidence.

## Reproducible data

- Source: [official OSM bounded map API](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map.json?bbox=-83.0183,39.9940,-83.0003,40.0045). A read-only query, no paid services. Overpass's primary endpoint rejected requests; a stalled mirror request was cancelled.
- Sanitized snapshot: [campus-data/osu-district.geojson](campus-data/osu-district.geojson), also pinned at `backend/app/data/osu-district.geojson` for offline application use.
- Exact retrieval time, request, SHA-256 hashes, feature counts, and omitted incomplete relations: [campus-data/osu-district-manifest.json](campus-data/osu-district-manifest.json).
- Reproducible sanitizer: [campus-data/build_expanded_snapshot.py](campus-data/build_expanded_snapshot.py). It accepts the downloaded OSM JSON and performs no network requests. Only whitelisted geometry/circulation/use tags survive. Addresses (including address-form building names), contacts and contributor metadata are omitted. Raw response stays outside the repository.
- Geographic snapshot: 10,251 features, approximately 3.73 MB. Whole selected ways may extend beyond the box; the loader deliberately clips scenery and excludes partial destination footprints. Seven multipolygon relations with missing members are recorded and omitted, not fabricated.
- Coordinate model: WGS84 local tangent approximation around (−83.0093, 39.99925), north up. Fixed 24.25 m per simulation unit. Runtime bounds and default camera are derived from the same transform.
- Attribution: **© OpenStreetMap contributors** — [copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). The existing dataset license applies to this expanded derivative too.

## Verification and limits

The geography regression tests verify source hash, connected walking network, directed driving connectivity, source access/one-way restrictions, complete house footprints, distinct doors, one-household house capacities, Greek/religious/dorm classifications, real campus relative positions, all wall/water-crossing intervals for authored entry and parking connectors, absence of unsupported solid-building interior routes, tree preservation, metric distances, and boundary commuter portals. Tests run entirely locally.

The nearest exterior connection is an **authored approach**, not a claim to know a real door, a legal public shortcut, or an accessible entrance. It is limited to 32 m, cannot cut through modeled walls, and remains clearly labeled. Family residents may use their own synthetic home's approach; this does not make that property public. The source does not establish real opening hours, current construction, curb/grade accessibility, traffic signals, turn restrictions, or live emergency conditions. Exact household allocation, job attendance, and crowd gathering must remain scenario assumptions until observational validation exists.

## Bounded activity classification follow-up (world version 2.1)

Lunch and coffee previously reused the generic commercial-building destination pool. That allowed meals at buildings with no food evidence and overlooked actual cafes whose building tag was simply `yes`. The same already-downloaded OSM response contains 79 public food POI nodes omitted by the first sanitizer; they are now preserved, with amenity/cuisine tags, in the pinned derivative. The raw response hash and retrieval time are unchanged. No new map request was made.

Food service now requires an explicit restaurant, fast-food, food-court, or cafe amenity on the building itself or a POI **contained by that existing footprint**, outside its courtyard holes. Coffee requires a cafe amenity. Explicit beverage-only cuisine such as bubble tea is not assumed to serve lunch or coffee. Generic retail, a bank, or a veterinary clinic is not food evidence. Each source POI is assigned once; there are no duplicate buildings, capacities, or invented adjacent storefronts. The resulting scene offers 41 food-bearing buildings and 15 coffee-bearing buildings. Activities carry the actual source venue name, while retaining the parent building ID and its one capacity.

The initial concern about **The View on High** and **Newport Music Hall** needs qualification: retained OSM POIs place **Yes Sushi** and **Chipotle**, respectively, inside those named footprints. The source supports a meal there, but displaying only the parent building name was misleading. The corrected activity names the restaurant. Similarly, Thompson Library carries Berry Cafe and Pomerene Hall carries Mirror Lake Eatery; no new building is invented. [OSU's dining directory](https://dining.osu.edu/dms/dininglocations/grid?area=dms&st=retail&x=39.997611&y=-83.014682) independently identifies campus dining locations, and [Berry Café's official description](https://www.dining.osu.edu/dininglocations/berry-caf/) confirms its light meals and coffee.

Seventeen food POIs are deliberately not attached: they lack a containing playable destination, occupy a clipped/unroutable building, fall in a garage, or lie inside a residential place whose public retail interior is not separately modeled. Examples include the ground-floor businesses in Statehouse Varsity, Marketplace on Neil in the non-playable Neil footprint, and Oxley's in Tuttle Garage. The code does not infer a public restaurant entrance through a private home or attach a nearby POI to whichever building happens to be closest. Venue hours, menus, service availability and membership restrictions are not validated live.

The residence classification also now covers the specific official South Campus catalog names, including **Fechko Alumnae Scholarship House, Hanley House, Pomerene House, and Worthington Building**. They are student residences, not classrooms or general study destinations. This is an exact named catalog override backed by [OSU Housing's residence list](https://housing.osu.edu/hms/roomsearch?campus=1&view=map); the word “House” alone never determines use.
