# Ohio State campus and University District

`osu-district.geojson` is the current pinned public OpenStreetMap extract; the exact request, retrieval date, sanitizer policy, and SHA-256 are in `osu-district-manifest.json`. `osu-core.geojson`, `osu-core-source.json` and `query.overpassql` retain the earlier academic-core snapshot for provenance. `OSM-LICENSE.md` provides attribution and the ODbL license link. Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors.

The application builds **osu-campus-district/2.1** locally without network calls. It clips the selected box (`-83.0183,39.9940,-83.0003,40.0045`) and projects WGS84 longitude/latitude into a north-up 68-unit world at 24.25 metres per unit. The approximately 1,537 × 1,166 metre scene includes the Oval, Mirror Lake, Ohio Union, South Campus dorms, and actual University District residential blocks east of High Street. It does not represent the entire Columbus campus. Old saved recordings retain their embedded city and coordinates. Version 2.1 also retains contained food-service POIs from the same downloaded response; actual venue metadata drives meal and coffee stops instead of assuming every commercial building sells food.

Walking and motor vehicles use separate graphs. Explicit access restrictions and one-way directions are respected; ordinary streets do not automatically become sidewalks. Unsupported interior walking corridors are trimmed at solid footprint boundaries before selecting connected networks. Complete building footprints receive distinct exterior doorway proxies; clipped buildings remain scenery. Short authored door/parking approaches cannot cross modeled walls or water. Four boundary portals represent off-map commuters. Households, door locations, capacities, pathway widths, travel speeds, and meeting spaces are educational assumptions rather than measured occupancy or verified access. The full audit and geographic evidence are in `docs/research/campus-geography-audit.md`.

The **osu-campus-life/1** population is wholly synthetic. Seeded roles, households, generative appearance descriptors, schedules, ownership and social links do not represent actual university residents. Families share trips; other household members can meet after separate activities. A vehicle moves only with a driver and conserved occupants, travels on its graph, parks, then releases riders to walk. The 24-hour recording has two-minute frames, five-second integration, and explicit paths/mode-transition times so accelerated playback does not skip boarding or alighting. Emergency runs initialize the same runtime at the recorded minute and issue destinations or holds without replacing its people or vehicles.

Source tags are incomplete. Unknown wheelchair access remains unknown; the model excludes mapped steps for wheelchair users but does not certify accessibility. Signal timing, turn restrictions, actual traffic demand, live access, elevation, interior layouts, opening hours and officially designated shelters are not modeled. Campus emergency suitability is an authored simulation overlay, not a real-world evacuation recommendation.
